Terms of use

Plain-language terms for using Nagarik. Please read them with the Privacy & Security centre.

Last updated 6 October 2026

Template terms for this preview. Have them reviewed by a qualified lawyer before launch.

1. What Nagarik is

Nagarik is an independent information and civic-reporting service. It is not a government website, does not issue documents, and does not collect government fees. Applications are made on official government portals.

2. Accuracy of information

We take service information from official sources and show when each guide was last reviewed. Rules change, and requirements can differ by state, so always confirm on the official portal before you apply. Nagarik is not responsible for decisions made by government departments.

3. AI guidance

Answers labelled “AI guidance” are generated summaries to help you understand a topic. They are not official advice and may contain mistakes. Check the linked official source before you act on them.

4. Your account

Keep your sign-in details private. You are responsible for activity on your account. Tell us straight away if you think someone else has used it.

5. Civic reports and community posts

Report real issues only, with your own photos. Do not post anyone’s personal details, offensive content or election campaigning. We may hide or remove content that breaks these rules, and suspend repeat offenders. Reports may be shared with the responsible municipal body.

6. Emergencies

Nagarik is not an emergency service. If life or property is in danger, call 112.

7. Privacy

How we collect, use and protect your data is explained in the Privacy & Security centre. We aim to comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

8. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. We will show the date of the latest version on this page and tell signed-in users about important changes.

9. Contact

Questions about these terms: legal@nagarik.example.
