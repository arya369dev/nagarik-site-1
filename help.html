Frequently asked questions

Reviewed by the Nagarik content team · 6 October 2026

What is Nagarik?

Nagarik is an independent digital platform that helps people in India find government services, understand the documents needed, apply on the official portal, report civic issues in their area and track progress in one place. It is not a government website.

How does Nagarik work?

Search for what you need in plain language. Nagarik explains the service, lists the usual documents, links you to the official portal to apply, and lets you save reference numbers so you can track every application and civic report from your dashboard.

How can I apply for a service?

Open the service page, check the document checklist, then press Apply on official portal. Nagarik prepares a checklist and takes you to the correct government portal, where the application is submitted and the fee, if any, is paid.

How do I report a civic issue?

Go to Report an issue, choose a category such as pothole or garbage, add a photo, confirm the location on the map and submit. You receive a tracking ID like NGK-24817 and updates as the issue moves from New to Resolved.

How can I track my application?

Enter your Nagarik tracking ID or a saved official reference number on the Track page. Civic reports show live status. For government applications, Nagarik shows the steps and links to the official tracking page.

What services are available through Nagarik?

Nagarik covers identity documents such as passport, Aadhaar update, PAN and voter ID; certificates such as birth, income and caste; transport services; welfare schemes; municipal services; and complaints such as RTI, CPGRAMS and cyber fraud reporting.

Is Nagarik a government website?

No. Nagarik is an independent civic-technology platform. It links to official government portals for applications and payments and clearly marks which information comes from official sources.

Is Nagarik free to use?

Yes. Searching, reporting civic issues and tracking are free. Government fees, where they apply, are paid directly on the official portal.

Which languages does Nagarik support?

Nagarik is designed for 13 languages: English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada, Gujarati, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, Urdu and Assamese. This preview has English and Hindi navigation.

How does Nagarik protect my data?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, documents are stored only with your consent, and you can download or delete your data at any time from the Privacy & Security centre.

Is the Nagarik AI assistant's advice official?

No. Answers from the assistant are labelled AI guidance. Information confirmed from a government source is labelled Official source with a link. Always confirm requirements on the official portal before applying.

What should I do in an emergency?

Call 112, India's single emergency number, for police, fire or ambulance. Nagarik's Emergency page lists other national helplines such as 1930 for cyber fraud and 1098 for children.

Guides

  • How to report a civic issue in under a minute
    Pick a category, add a photo, confirm the pin and submit. You get an NGK tracking ID straight away.
  • Documents usually needed for a passport
    Proof of address and date of birth, plus your old passport for a re-issue. Always confirm on Passport Seva before you apply.
  • Updating your Aadhaar address online
    Log in to myAadhaar with OTP, upload a valid proof of address and keep your Update Request Number to track it.
  • Tracking applications and reports in one place
    Use your NGK ID for civic reports, and save official reference numbers so Nagarik can remind you to check their status.
  • Lost money to online fraud? Act in the first hours
    Call 1930, file a complaint on cybercrime.gov.in and inform your bank. Speed improves the chance of recovery.
  • How to download or delete your Nagarik data
    Open the Privacy & Security centre to export everything we hold or permanently delete your account.
