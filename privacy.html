Privacy & security centre

Your data, your control

We collect the minimum, encrypt everything, and never sell personal data. Designed to align with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Last updated 6 October 2026

Encrypted everywhere

TLS 1.3 for every connection and AES-256 encryption for stored data and documents, with keys in a managed key service.

Secure sign-in

Mobile OTP with optional passkeys and two-step verification. Sessions expire and can be ended from any device.

Safe file uploads

File type and size checks, virus scanning, and removal of hidden location data from photos before storage.

Role-based access

Ward officers see only their ward’s reports; personal contact details are masked. Every access is logged.

Abuse protection

Rate limiting, bot detection and input validation on every form and API to stop spam and attacks.

Data minimisation

We ask only for what a task needs, and delete inactive accounts’ data after 24 months.
