What is Nagarik?

Nagarik is an independent digital platform that helps people in India find government services, understand the documents needed, apply on the official portal, report civic issues in their area and track progress in one place. It is not a government website.

Quick answers

Common questions

How does Nagarik work?

Search for what you need in plain language. Nagarik explains the service, lists the usual documents, links you to the official portal to apply, and lets you save reference numbers so you can track every application and civic report from your dashboard.

How can I apply for a service?

Open the service page, check the document checklist, then press Apply on official portal. Nagarik prepares a checklist and takes you to the correct government portal, where the application is submitted and the fee, if any, is paid.

How do I report a civic issue?

Go to Report an issue, choose a category such as pothole or garbage, add a photo, confirm the location on the map and submit. You receive a tracking ID like NGK-24817 and updates as the issue moves from New to Resolved.

How can I track my application?

Enter your Nagarik tracking ID or a saved official reference number on the Track page. Civic reports show live status. For government applications, Nagarik shows the steps and links to the official tracking page.

What services are available through Nagarik?

Nagarik covers identity documents such as passport, Aadhaar update, PAN and voter ID; certificates such as birth, income and caste; transport services; welfare schemes; municipal services; and complaints such as RTI, CPGRAMS and cyber fraud reporting.
