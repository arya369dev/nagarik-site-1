Emergency information

In an emergency, call 112

112 connects police, fire and ambulance anywhere in India, from any phone, even without a SIM balance.

Last updated 6 October 2026

112
All emergencies

Police · Fire · Ambulance. Free from any phone. The 112 India app can also share your location.

What is the emergency number in India?

112 is India’s single emergency number for police, fire and ambulance. Specialised helplines below handle women’s safety, children, cyber fraud and senior citizens.

Source: Emergency Response Support System, Ministry of Home Affairs · 112.gov.in ↗

Stay safe

  • Save 112 and a family contact on speed dial.
  • During floods, avoid underpasses and open drains.
  • Banks and government officials never ask for your OTP or PIN.
  • For online fraud, call 1930 within the first hours.
