Service directory

Public services, explained clearly

What each service is, who provides it, the documents you usually need, and the official portal where you apply. No jargon.

Last updated 6 October 2026

Nagarik guides you to official portals. Applications and fees are always submitted on the government website. Requirements can vary by state: check the official link before applying.

Passport (fresh or re-issue)

Identity & documents · पासपोर्ट
Official link checked 6 October 2026

Apply online on Passport Seva, pay the fee, then visit a Passport Seva Kendra for biometrics and document checks. Police verification follows.

Documents, steps & timeline

Documents usually required

  • Proof of present address (e.g. Aadhaar, utility bill, bank passbook)
  • Proof of date of birth (e.g. birth certificate, school leaving certificate)
  • Old passport, for re-issue
  • Annexures where applicable (e.g. for minors)

Steps

  1. Register on Passport Seva
  2. Fill the application form
  3. Pay and book an appointment
  4. Visit the Passport Seva Kendra
  5. Police verification
  6. Passport dispatched by Speed Post

Time: Varies by city and police verification · Mode: Online + in-person visit

Provided by Ministry of External Affairs · Source: Passport Seva ↗ Start application

Aadhaar address update

Identity & documents · आधार पता अपडेट
Official link checked 6 October 2026

Update your address online on myAadhaar with a valid proof of address, or visit an Aadhaar Seva Kendra for biometric or mobile-number changes.

Documents, steps & timeline

Documents usually required

  • Valid proof of address in your name
  • Aadhaar number with registered mobile for OTP

Steps

  1. Log in to myAadhaar with OTP
  2. Choose address update
  3. Upload proof of address
  4. Note the Update Request Number (URN)
  5. Track the URN until updated

Time: Typically a few working days to weeks · Mode: Online or Aadhaar Seva Kendra

Provided by Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) · Source: myAadhaar ↗ Start application

PAN card (new or e-PAN)

Identity & documents · पैन कार्ड
Official link checked 6 October 2026

Get an instant e-PAN using Aadhaar-based e-KYC on the Income Tax e-Filing portal, or apply for a physical PAN through the authorised PAN service agencies.

Documents, steps & timeline

Documents usually required

  • Aadhaar with linked mobile number (for instant e-PAN)
  • Identity, address and date-of-birth proof for other applications

Steps

  1. Open Instant e-PAN on the e-Filing portal
  2. Verify with Aadhaar OTP
  3. Download e-PAN

Time: Instant e-PAN is usually issued quickly · Mode: Online

Provided by Income Tax Department · Source: e-Filing portal ↗ Start application

Voter ID registration (Form 6)

Identity & documents · मतदाता पहचान पत्र
Official link checked 6 October 2026

Citizens aged 18 or more register as a new voter with Form 6 on the Voters' Service Portal. A Booth Level Officer may verify your details.

Documents, steps & timeline

Documents usually required

  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Proof of age
  • Proof of ordinary residence

Steps

  1. Sign up on the Voters' Service Portal
  2. Fill Form 6
  3. Upload photo and documents
  4. Track with the reference number

Time: Depends on verification · Mode: Online

Provided by Election Commission of India · Source: Voters' Service Portal ↗ Start application

Birth certificate

Certificates · जन्म प्रमाण पत्र
Official link checked 6 October 2026

Births are registered with the local registrar, usually by the hospital or the family. Registration within 21 days is free under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act.

Documents, steps & timeline

Documents usually required

  • Hospital discharge or birth report
  • Parents' identity proof
  • Address proof

Steps

  1. Confirm the birth was reported to the registrar
  2. Apply on CRS or at the municipal office
  3. Download or collect the certificate

Time: Varies by local body · Mode: Online or municipal office

Provided by Office of the Registrar General, India (Civil Registration System) · Source: CRS ↗ Start application

Income certificate

Certificates · आय प्रमाण पत्र
Official link checked 6 October 2026

Income certificates are issued by state governments, usually through the state e-District or citizen-service portal or a Common Service Centre.

Documents, steps & timeline

Documents usually required

  • Identity proof
  • Address proof
  • Income proof or self-declaration as required by your state
  • Ration card, if your state asks for it

Steps

  1. Find your state portal
  2. Fill the application
  3. Upload documents and pay the state fee
  4. Track and download the signed certificate

Time: Set by each state's service guarantee · Mode: Online or CSC

Provided by State Revenue Department · Source: Your state's e-District / service portal ↗ Start application

Learner's & driving licence

Transport · ड्राइविंग लाइसेंस
Official link checked 6 October 2026

Apply for a learner's licence on Sarathi, pass the learner's test, and after the waiting period book a driving test at your RTO for the permanent licence.

Documents, steps & timeline

Documents usually required

  • Proof of age
  • Proof of address
  • Medical certificate (Form 1 / 1A) where applicable
  • Photograph and signature

Steps

  1. Choose your state on Sarathi
  2. Apply for learner's licence
  3. Take the learner's test
  4. Book driving test after waiting period
  5. Receive driving licence

Time: Several weeks end to end · Mode: Online + RTO visit

Provided by Ministry of Road Transport & Highways · Source: Sarathi (Parivahan) ↗ Start application

Vehicle registration services

Transport · वाहन पंजीकरण
Official link checked 6 October 2026

Transfer of ownership, address change on RC, duplicate RC and other vehicle services are handled on Vahan for most states.

Documents, steps & timeline

Documents usually required

  • Insurance and PUC
  • Identity and address proof
  • Forms specific to the service

Steps

  1. Select the RTO and service
  2. Fill details and pay

  4. Visit RTO if asked

Time: Varies by RTO · Mode: Online + RTO

Provided by Ministry of Road Transport & Highways · Source: Vahan (Parivahan) ↗ Start application

Ration card

Welfare & health · राशन कार्ड
Official link checked 6 October 2026

Ration cards are issued by state food departments. Under One Nation One Ration Card, eligible beneficiaries can collect entitlements from any fair price shop.

Documents, steps & timeline

Documents usually required

  • Identity proof of the head of family
  • Address proof
  • Family member details and Aadhaar

Steps

  1. Visit your state food portal
  2. Apply for a new card or a member addition
  3. Verification
  4. Card issued

Time: Set by each state · Mode: Online or office

Provided by Department of Food & Public Distribution and state food departments · Source: NFSA / state food portal ↗ Start application

Provided by National Health Authority · Source: Beneficiary portal ↗ Start application

Documents, steps & timeline

RTI application

Complaints & rights · सूचना का अधिकार
Official link checked 6 October 2026

Ask a central public authority for information under the RTI Act, 2005. State authorities have their own RTI portals or offline process.

Documents, steps & timeline

Documents usually required

  • Your question, clearly worded
  • BPL proof if claiming fee exemption

Steps

  1. Choose the public authority
  2. Write the request
  3. Pay the fee
  4. Track the reply

Time: Reply due within 30 days under the Act · Mode: Online

Provided by Department of Personnel & Training (central public authorities) · Source: RTI Online ↗ Start application

Cyber crime & online fraud report

Complaints & rights · साइबर अपराध रिपोर्ट
Official link checked 6 October 2026

Report online financial fraud immediately on helpline 1930 and file the complaint on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

Documents, steps & timeline

Documents usually required

  • Transaction IDs and screenshots
  • Bank or UPI details
  • Suspect's phone, URL or profile, if known

Steps

  1. Call 1930 as soon as possible
  2. File complaint online
  3. Note the acknowledgement number
  4. Follow up with your bank

Time: Report within hours for the best chance of recovery · Mode: Phone + online

Provided by Ministry of Home Affairs (I4C) · Source: National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal ↗ Start application
