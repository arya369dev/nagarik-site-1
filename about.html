About Nagarik

Built for every nagarik

Nagarik means ‘citizen’ in Hindi and many Indian languages. We make public services easier to find, understand and complete.

Last updated 6 October 2026

Nagarik in one paragraph

Nagarik is an independent digital platform that helps people in India find government services, understand the documents needed, apply on the official portal, report civic issues in their area and track progress in one place. It is not a government website. Nagarik’s tagline is “Simple services. Smarter citizen experience.”

What Nagarik is

  • A guide to public services in plain language
  • A tool to report and track local civic issues
  • A dashboard to follow applications in one place
  • A multilingual, accessible, low-data experience

What Nagarik is not

  • A government website or department
  • An issuer of documents or certificates
  • A collector of government fees
  • A replacement for 112 in emergencies

How we source information

  1. Official first. Every service guide links to the ministry, department or state portal that provides it.
  2. Dated. Each page shows when it was last reviewed. We re-check high-traffic services monthly.
  3. Labelled. We separate official information, our own help content and AI-generated guidance with visible labels.
  4. Correctable. Anyone can report an error; corrections are logged.
